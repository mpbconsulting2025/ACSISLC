The Johari Window

Interaction between two or more people depends on the extent of openness as well as the context in which it takes place. This workbook keeps each person's choices separate before comparing them.

Why this may help

The Johari Window can help you practise giving and receiving feedback. Separate lists show what you think describes you and what each observer thinks, so agreement and difference are visible.

1. SELF
Which of these words describe you? Make your choices without seeing anyone else's.
2. OBSERVERS
Ask one or two trusted people which words they think best describe you.
3. JOHARI WINDOW
Compare only after each relevant list has been completed and confirmed.
