The ACSIS model

ACSIS 3C

Move from understanding to brave action, supported by connection. Work the four stages in order; each one makes the next possible.

Why this may help

Helps you move from understanding to action without losing support. It checks that clarity, courage and connection are all present.

Clarity see it clearly
Courage act on it honestly
Connection don’t do it alone
C
Clarity
What is happening, what matters and what needs to change?
· Describe the situation as a neutral observer would.
· What matters most to you in this?
· What, specifically, needs to be different?
C
Courage
What honest conversation, choice or action is needed?
· What have you been avoiding saying or deciding?
· What is the brave version of your next step?
· What is the cost of not doing it?
C
Connection
Who, what or where will provide support and accountability?
· Who will you tell, and when?
· Who has done this before and could help?
· How will you be held to it?
S
Sustainability
What makes the change realistic and maintainable?
· What would make this easy to keep doing on a bad week?
· What will you stop to make room?
· How will you know in three months that it stuck?

Clarity without courage is insight; courage without connection burns out. Check all three before closing.
