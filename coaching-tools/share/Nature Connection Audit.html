Nature Connection Audit

Notice how nature is present in your life and choose one realistic way to deepen that relationship. Nature can mean a park, water, weather, wildlife, a garden, a houseplant or the view from a window.

Why this may help

Looking at different pathways to nature can reveal accessible forms of connection that are already present or easy to test.

This is not a health assessment or treatment. Adapt every idea for your mobility, energy, sensory needs, safety, access, culture and local environment.

Five pathways to connection

Rate each pathway from 1 (rarely present) to 5 (strongly present) over the last two weeks.
