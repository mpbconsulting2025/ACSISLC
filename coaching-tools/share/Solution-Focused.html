Coaching approach · Clarity & Courage

Solution-focused conversation

You do not need to understand a problem fully to solve it. This sheet looks for the future you want, the times it is already partly happening, and the smallest next step.

Why this may help

Shifts attention from analysing the problem indefinitely to noticing exceptions, strengths and the next useful step.

1

Describe the preferred future in detail, as if it had already arrived.

2

Hunt for exceptions and strengths. When is the problem absent or smaller? What are you doing then?

3

Scale and step. Place yourself on 0–10, then name a step that moves you one point.

The miracle question
Suppose tonight, while you sleep, a miracle happens and this is completely resolved. You don’t know it happened. What is the first thing you notice tomorrow that tells you? What else? Who else notices?

What were you doing differently then?

What would someone who knows you well say you bring?

10 = the miracle has happened. 0 = the worst it has been.
