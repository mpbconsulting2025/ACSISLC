Session opener · Clarity

STOKeRS

The first five minutes decide the next fifty-five. Seven questions that bound the conversation and right-size it to the time available. From Claire Pedrick, Simplifying Coaching.

Why this may help

Helps open and right-size a coaching conversation so both client and coach know what would make the session useful.

S
Subject
What do we need to think about today?
T
Time
Given we have X minutes, what about that should we focus on?
O
Outcome
What would you like to be different by the end of our session?
K
Know
How will you know you’ve achieved what you needed from this time?
e
Essence
What makes this meaningful or important? What is at the heart of this issue?
R
Role
How should we approach this together?
S
Start
Where shall we begin?

Tip. Ask, then wait. The client’s first answer to Subject is rarely the real one; Essence usually finds it. If Time and Outcome do not fit together, renegotiate the Outcome, not the Time.
