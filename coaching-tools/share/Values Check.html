Values check

Values are what you would protect if you had to choose. When daily life honours them, decisions feel clean; when it doesn’t, something nags. This sheet finds the gap.

Why this may help

Clarifying values can explain why a decision feels right, conflicted or draining, and can guide a more aligned choice.

1

Tick every value that resonates. Go quickly; first instinct. Add your own in the blank spaces.

2

Choose your top five. If two are similar, keep the one you would defend. Then score how well life honours each right now.

3

Look at the lowest score. That is where the friction lives. Decide one change that would raise it.
