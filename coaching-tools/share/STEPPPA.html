Coaching model · Clarity & Courage

STEPPPA

Angus McLeod’s model puts emotion at the centre of goal-setting, because a target you don’t feel anything about is a target you won’t pursue. Use it when feelings are driving or blocking the situation.

Why this may help

Makes space for emotion and perception before planning, which can reveal what is really affecting follow-through.

1

Get the subject and target clear, then stop and check how you feel about the target. Low feeling means wrong target.

2

Widen perception before planning: other views, other choices, the bigger picture.

3

Plan with a pace you can keep, then act, and adapt when reality answers back.

S
Subject
What is the issue, and what is its context? Why bring it now?
T
Target
What is the goal? Make it specific enough to picture.
E
Emotion
How do you feel about this target? What emotions are driving the current behaviour?
{{ v.feel }}
How strongly do you want this? 1–10
P
Perception
What is the broader perspective? How would others see it? What choices does that open up?
P
Plan
What is the plan? Which steps, in what order? What is the price of it?
P
Pace
What is the timeline? Which milestones keep it moving without burning out?
A
Action / Adapt
What is the first action? When will you review and adjust?

If motivation drops during Plan or Pace, go back to Emotion. STEPPPA: Angus McLeod, Performance Coaching (2003).
