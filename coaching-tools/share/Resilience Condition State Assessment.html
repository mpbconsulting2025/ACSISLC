A quick check of your current capacity, not your character. Notice what your system is telling you, then choose a proportionate response for today.

Why this may help

A brief capacity check can help you respond earlier to strain and choose protection, recovery or support.

This is a reflective coaching tool, not a clinical assessment. If you or someone else is in danger, call 999 or go to A&E. If you need urgent mental health help but it is not an emergency, use NHS 111 online or call 111 and select the mental health option.
