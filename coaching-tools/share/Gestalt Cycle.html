Gestalt Cycle of Experience

Every need, goal or task moves through the same loop, from a first stirring to completion and rest. Feeling stuck usually means the loop has been interrupted somewhere. Find where.

Why this may help

Helps identify where awareness, choice or action is getting interrupted so you can work with the next stage without forcing an answer.

1

Bring one situation to mind where you feel stuck or something feels unfinished.

2

Walk the cycle and tick the stage where your energy stops. The table shows how each stage typically gets interrupted.

3

Ask what would complete it. One small step that moves you to the next stage.

Stuck?StageHealthy flowTypical interruption
1. Sensation
Something stirs: a feeling, a nag, a physical signal.
Numbing or ignoring the signal; too busy to notice.
2. Awareness
You name what the sensation is about and what you need.
Denial or distraction; changing the subject.
3. Mobilisation
Energy gathers; you start to want to do something.
Overthinking; waiting for certainty before moving.
4. Action
You move: a conversation, a decision, a first step.
Doing something else instead; busy but not on this.
5. Contact
You fully meet the situation, person or task.
Holding back; going through the motions.
6. Satisfaction
You take in what happened and feel the completion.
Rushing on; “not good enough”; guilt about the need.
7. Withdrawal
You let go and rest, ready for the next cycle.
Can’t switch off; keep replaying it.

The cycle is a lens for listening, not a script. Notice the client’s language for the stage they keep returning to.
