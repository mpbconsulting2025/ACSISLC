SMART action

Turn an intention into an action you can actually start, and know when you have finished. Write the rough version first, test it against the five checks, then rewrite it.

Why this may help

Turns a broad intention into an action that is specific enough to start, track and review.

S
Specific
What exactly will you do? Who is involved, where does it happen?
M
Measurable
How will you know it is done? What number or evidence counts?
A
Achievable
Is this realistic with the time, energy and support you have? What would make it so?
R
Relevant
Why does this matter to you now? Which bigger goal does it serve?
T
Time-bound
By when? What is the first date in your diary?

Confidence below 7? Shrink the action or the deadline until it moves up. Small and certain beats big and hopeful.
