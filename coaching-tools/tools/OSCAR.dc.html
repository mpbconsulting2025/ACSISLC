Coaching model · Clarity

OSCAR

A GROW-style structure that adds a weighing of consequences before you choose. Use it when there are several routes and the cost of each matters.

Why this may help

Useful when you need to weigh choices and consequences before committing to action and review.

1

Outcome first, so the conversation has a destination.

2

Situation, then choices with consequences. For each option: what happens if you do, and if you don’t?

3

Commit to actions, then agree how and when you will review.

O
Outcome
What do you want to achieve, long-term and from today? How will you know you have it?
S
Situation
What is happening now? What have you tried? Who else is involved?
C
Choices & consequences - list up to three options and what each would lead to
A
Actions
Which option? What exactly will you do, by when? What support do you need?
R
Review
When will we check progress? What will tell us it worked?

OSCAR: Whittleworth & Gilbert (2002). Its strength is the Consequences column; do not skip it.
