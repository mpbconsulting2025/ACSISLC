Coaching model · Clarity

T-GROW

A five-step route from a topic to a first action. Work top to bottom; if the conversation drifts, return to the Topic and Goal.

Why this may help

Gives a conversation a route from topic and goal through reality and options to a clear next step.

1

Name the topic and goal in one sentence each. If you can’t, that is the work.

2

Describe reality honestly before options. Most people skip this and choose too early.

3

List options before judging them, then commit to one first step with a date.

T
G
Goal
What do you want to achieve?
How will you know you have it?
What makes it matter now?
R
Reality
What is happening right now?
What have you tried already?
What is getting in the way?
O
Options
What else could you do?
What would you tell a friend?
If constraints vanished, what then?
W
Way forward
What will you do first?
When exactly?
What might stop you, and what then?

T-GROW adds Topic before Goal, Reality, Options and Way Forward. Keep the topic and goal visible throughout.
