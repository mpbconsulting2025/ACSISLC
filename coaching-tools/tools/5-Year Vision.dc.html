ACSIS 5-Year Vision

When you can see your future clearly, you subconsciously move towards it every day. Give yourself at least 30 minutes, on your own, in a quiet reflective space.

Why this may help

Looking further ahead can make current choices clearer. Working backwards turns a distant picture into steps you can test now.

1
Prepare. Give yourself at least 30 minutes. Make sure you’re in a quiet reflective space, on your own - perhaps take some deep breaths and clear your mind of clutter. Prepare to think boldly.
2
Imagine it’s today, in 5 years’ time. Write that future date down. Then write what you’re doing that day, from the moment you wake up to the moment you go to sleep. Dream big. Dream in detail. What if you got everything you ever wanted?
Get specific
Reflect deeply and answer these:

A note as you write. You might surprise yourself with what appears on the page. That’s normal. We rarely let ourselves dream honestly and freely. Keep writing until you have nothing left to say. No filters. No limits. No other desires left unspoken.

From dream to Year 1

In each of the 5 years, what one thing MUST you do to make your dream a reality?

Work backwards from Year 5. Write each year out with the top 2–3 things that must be true to reach the goal.

Example · “I want to retire in 5 years”
Year 5 I need £250,000 to retire, mortgage paid off, car paid off.
Year 4 I need £200,000 - therefore I need to increase salary (new job, second job, reduce spending, invest, downsize, what are my options).
Year 3 I need £150,000 - sell car and get a smaller, cheaper, more efficient one; sell sports kit I no longer use (can do that now?); ensure mortgage is paid off.
Year 2 I need £100,000 - assess second job, can investments be better, can I cut subscriptions and spending to save more?
Year 1 I need £50,000 - I have £20k, therefore I need to reduce spending, get a part-time job or promotion, save more, start an ISA, invest.

Review. Re-read your vision in a few weeks and add detail, amend, refine. Then re-read it every 6 months, adding new details as you learn more about yourself. Put this review in your calendar now.
Why it works. When you can see your future clearly, you subconsciously move towards it every day. You’ll make micro-decisions that nudge you in the right direction. It’s like setting a destination in maps rather than wandering without direction. At every intersection, you know exactly where to turn.
