Coaching framework · Clarity & Courage

ACHIEVE

Use this seven-stage framework when a challenge has several moving parts, stakeholders or consequences. Work through the stages in order, while staying responsive to what the client needs.

Why this may help

Useful when a challenge is complex or the first goal is not yet the right one. It slows the conversation enough to explore options and build a plan you own.

Start with the real situation.
Separate facts, interpretations, people and pressures.
Generate before judging.
Make room for possibilities before comparing them.
Finish with ownership.
Test the plan and decide how momentum will be sustained.
A

Assess the current situation

What is happening? What are the facts, relationships, pressures, assumptions and available resources?

C

Create goals

What broad outcome does the client want from this situation and from the coaching conversation?

H

Hone goals

Make the goal precise. What would success look like, for whom, by when and with what evidence?

I

Identify options

Generate several possible routes before selecting one. Include small, bold and collaborative options.

ACHIEVE · Choose and commit

Turn the thinking into movement

Compare the strongest options, test what is realistic and build the support that will keep the plan moving.

E

Evaluate options

Compare benefits, risks, effort, values, resources and stakeholder impact. What trade-offs matter?

V

Validate the plan

Check sequence, ownership, timing and readiness. What could derail the plan, and how will that be handled?

E

Encourage momentum

Identify the first move, support, accountability and review points that will sustain action after the session.

This worksheet follows the wording in the linked TPC Coaching Academy overview. ACHIEVE was developed by Sabine Dembkowski and Fiona Eldridge. Published versions use some different stage labels.
