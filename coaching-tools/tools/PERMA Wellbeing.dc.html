PERMA wellbeing check

Use PERMA to take a reflective snapshot of five routes into wellbeing, notice what is supporting you and choose one practical experiment. It is a coaching conversation tool, not a diagnostic or clinical assessment.

Why this may help

Offers a structured wellbeing snapshot across five areas, helping you choose one practical focus without treating the result as a diagnosis.

1

Choose one recent period, such as the last four weeks. Score every pillar against the same period: 1 means barely present and 10 means strongly present.

2

Add real evidence. Note the people, places, activities and habits that currently support or reduce each pillar.

3

Choose one useful focus. It need not be the lowest score. Pick the area where one small action could make a meaningful difference.

P
Positive emotion
Pleasure, gratitude, hope and contentment. This means having access to positive feelings, not being positive all the time.
