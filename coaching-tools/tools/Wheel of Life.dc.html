Build your Wheel of Life

Name the areas that matter in your life, then score each one twice: how satisfied you feel now and how important it is to you. The space between the two scores helps you choose where to focus.

Why this may help

Seeing satisfaction beside importance highlights where a gap matters to you, rather than assuming the lowest score should always come first.

1

Choose your areas. Start with four, use the suggested eight, or add your own.

2

Score both scales. Satisfaction is how life feels now. Importance is how much the area matters.

3

Notice the gaps. A larger importance score can point towards a useful coaching focus.

What is the wheel telling you?

There is no perfect shape and no correct score. Use the wheel to notice what matters, what is working, and where one small change could help.
