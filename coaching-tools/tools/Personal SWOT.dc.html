Personal SWOT

SWOT means Strength, Weakness, Opportunity and Threat. Use the four boxes to examine one role, decision or goal. Strengths and weaknesses are internal; opportunities and threats are external.

Why this may help

Brings internal strengths and weaknesses together with external opportunities and threats, making a situation easier to plan around.

Helpful
Harmful
Internal
S · Strength
What I bring
· What do you do well, and what do others rely on you for?
· Which achievements are you proudest of?
· What resources, skills or contacts do you have?
W · Weakness
What holds me back
· What do you avoid or find draining?
· Where has feedback pointed more than once?
· What gaps in skill, time or confidence show up?
External
O · Opportunity
What is open to me
· What is changing around you that you could use?
· Who could help, and who needs what you offer?
· Which strength could you apply somewhere new?
T · Threat
What could get in the way
· What obstacles or competition do you face?
· Which limitation could a threat expose?
· What happens if nothing changes?

Ask for evidence in every box. “I’m good with people” becomes “three people came to me for advice this month.”
