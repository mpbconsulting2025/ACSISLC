Scaling questions

A 0 to 10 scale turns a fuzzy feeling into something you can look at, talk about and move. There is no right number. The value is in what the number tells you.

Why this may help

A number can make change easier to notice and helps identify what is already working and what one point of progress would look like.

1

Define the scale. 10 is the situation exactly as you want it; 0 is the worst it has been.

2

Place yourself, then place where you want to be. Notice the gap, and what you already have.

3

Move one point, not ten. Describe what one point higher would look like and do that.
