Coaching model · Connection

CLEAR

Peter Hawkins’ model for conversations where trust matters as much as the outcome. It starts with an explicit contract and ends with a review of the relationship, not just the task.

Why this may help

Creates a clear beginning, middle and end for a coaching conversation. It also makes space to review what changed.

1

Contract before content. Agree what you both want from the time and how you will work together.

2

Listen and explore before acting. The client should hear their own thinking reflected back before choosing.

3

Review the process too. What helped, what didn’t, what to do differently next time.

C
Contracting
Together
What do you want from this session? What would make it worthwhile? How should we work together, and what are the boundaries?
L
Listening
Coach
Reflect back what you hear: content, feeling, pattern. What is being said underneath the words?
E
Exploring
Together
What is really going on? What impact is this having on you and others? What other perspectives are there?
A
Action
Client
What will you do, and by when? What is your first step? What support do you need?
R
Review
Together
What has been useful today? What did we do well together? What would you like different next time?

CLEAR: Peter Hawkins (1980s). Especially suited to sensitive topics, new relationships and leadership development.
