Goal and behaviour tool · Clarity & Courage

WOOP

Turn an important wish into a realistic response to the main inner obstacle. Complete the four steps in order and pause long enough to picture the outcome and obstacle before writing.

Why this may help

Helps connect an important wish with the inner obstacle most likely to interrupt it, then creates a practical if-then response.

Choose one wish.
It should matter, stretch you and still feel possible.
Look inward for the obstacle.
Name the habit, feeling, thought or response within you.
Make one if-then plan.
Choose a specific response you can use when the obstacle appears.
W

Wish

What do you really want to do or experience? Choose something important, challenging and achievable.

O

Outcome

What would be the best result, and how would it feel? Picture it vividly before writing.

O

Obstacle

What is the main inner obstacle? Look beneath external barriers for a habit, belief, emotion or impulse you can influence.

P

Plan

Choose one short, concrete and doable action or helpful thought. Link it directly to the obstacle.
