Why might this help?

When things feel busy, choosing a single need can reduce the effort of deciding what to do next. These tools offer structure, not judgement. Use what helps and leave what does not.

Build your task list, choose a session or use the Pomodoro setup, then press Start session. Optional breathing comes first. Focus blocks and breaks then change automatically until the session is complete.

Breathe

Follow a rhythm that feels comfortable

Keep your breathing easy. Stop or return to your usual breathing if you feel uncomfortable or light-headed.

How do I use this?

Choose a rhythm, read its ? guide and set a length. Press Start breathing and follow the words, number and expanding circle. Keep every breath easy. Pause or stop if the pace is not comfortable.

Best-practice guidance
  • Begin with five minutes and keep each breath light and comfortable.
  • Breathe through your nose if that is comfortable, without taking the deepest breath possible.
  • Your exact resonance rate can differ. Finding it precisely normally requires reliable HRV measurement.
  • Stop and return to normal breathing if you feel dizzy, short of breath or uncomfortable.

Meditate

Set aside a little quiet

Choose a time, an anchor and optional background sound. There is no voice and nothing to achieve.

How do I use this?

Choose one guidance style and a session length. Press Begin meditation, follow each on-screen direction and return to it gently whenever your attention moves elsewhere. Nothing needs to feel a particular way.

Soundscapes

Build the space you need

Play a ready-made mix or combine sounds yourself. There is no timer, so the sound continues until you stop it.

How do I use this?

Choose a ready-made space or build your own background mix. A pure tone is optional and has its own volume. Start quietly, listen for comfort rather than an effect, and stop any sound that feels distracting.
